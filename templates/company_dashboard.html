PREVENTION OF SEXUAL HARASSMENT (POSH) POLICY

{{ posh_policy.company_name }}

{{ posh_policy.registered_address }}{% if posh_policy.district_name %}, {{ posh_policy.district_name }}{% endif %}

Effective Date: {{ posh_policy.effective_date }}  |  HR Email: {{ posh_policy.hr_email }}

1. Policy Statement

{{ posh_policy.company_name }} is firmly committed to providing a work environment that is free from any form of sexual harassment and discrimination for all persons associated with the organization. The Company has a zero-tolerance approach towards sexual harassment and any behavior that violates the dignity of any employee or associate.

The Company strictly prohibits any form of discrimination or harassment against any employee, visitor, contract employee, intern, trainee, consultant, vendor, or applicant for employment because of the individual’s race, color, religion, gender, real or suspected sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, national origin, region, age, disability, marital status, pregnancy, or any other personal characteristic protected by law.

Any use of company facilities, premises, systems or resources (including email, messaging systems, notice boards, documents, online collaboration tools, or social media used in a work-related context) as a vehicle for harassment is strictly prohibited and will be treated as misconduct.

2. Objective

The objective of this Policy is to:

  • Prevent, prohibit and redress sexual harassment of women at the workplace in accordance with The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 and the rules framed thereunder (“POSH Act”).
  • Provide a safe working environment where every individual is treated with dignity and respect and is able to work without fear of prejudice, gender bias, intimidation, or sexual harassment.
  • Lay down a clear and fair procedure for receiving, investigating, and resolving complaints of sexual harassment, and for taking appropriate disciplinary and remedial action.

This Policy is deemed to be part of the terms of employment/service/engagement of all persons covered by it.

3. Scope

This Policy applies to:

  • All employees of {{ posh_policy.company_name }} (whether permanent, temporary, probationary, trainee, intern, part-time, or contractual).
  • Persons engaged through contractors, consultants, vendors, or service providers.
  • Visitors, clients, customers, and any other third parties who access the workplace.

Workplace: For the purpose of this Policy, “workplace” includes:

  • All offices, branches, units, factories, warehouses, or project sites of {{ posh_policy.company_name }}, whether permanent, temporary, or shifting.
  • Any place visited by an employee arising out of or during the course of employment, including client locations, training programs, off-sites, business trips, conferences, and work-related social events.
  • Virtual/online workplaces, including online meetings, chats, emails, or any digital communication platforms used for work.
4. Policy Overview and Commitment

{{ posh_policy.company_name }} is committed to:

  • Providing a gender-sensitive, safe, and inclusive workplace free from sexual harassment, intimidation, and abuse.
  • Taking prompt and appropriate action against any form of sexual harassment, whether alleged against an employee, supervisor, manager, or any other person at the workplace.
  • Sensitizing all employees through regular awareness programs, communication, and training on the POSH Act and this Policy.
  • Ensuring that the process for raising concerns and complaints is simple, accessible, fair, and confidential.
  • Ensuring that no victim, witness, or any person who assists in a complaint is subjected to retaliation or victimization.
5. Internal Committee (IC)

In accordance with the POSH Act, {{ posh_policy.company_name }} has constituted an Internal Committee (IC) to receive and redress complaints of sexual harassment at the workplace.

  • The Presiding Officer shall be a woman employed at a senior level at the workplace.
  • At least one-half of the IC members shall be women.
  • The External Member shall be from an NGO or association committed to the cause of women, or a person familiar with issues relating to sexual harassment.
5.2 Term of the Internal Committee
  • Each member of the IC shall hold office for a term not exceeding three (3) years from the date of their nomination.
  • The Company may, at its discretion, reconstitute the IC earlier or reappoint members based on requirement, experience, and compliance needs.
5.3 Display and Communication
  • The names and contact details of the IC members will be displayed at prominent places in the workplace and on the intranet/communication channels.
  • Employees will be informed about the IC and the procedure for raising complaints during induction and through periodic communications.
6. Definitions

6.1 Aggrieved Woman: A woman, of any age and whether employed or not, who alleges to have been subjected to any act of sexual harassment by the respondent at the workplace.

6.2 Respondent: A person against whom the aggrieved woman has made a complaint of sexual harassment under this Policy.

6.3 Sexual Harassment: "Sexual Harassment" includes any unwelcome act or behavior (whether directly or by implication), such as:

  • Physical contact and advances.
  • Demand or request of sexual favors.
  • Making sexually colored remarks.
  • Showing pornography.
  • Any other unwelcome physical, verbal, non-verbal or written conduct of a sexual nature.

The following circumstances, among others, may amount to sexual harassment if they occur in connection with any act or behavior of sexual nature:

  • Implied or explicit promise of preferential treatment in employment.
  • Implied or explicit threat of detrimental treatment in employment.
  • Implied or explicit threat about the present or future employment status.
  • Interference with work or creating an intimidating, offensive, or hostile work environment.
  • Humiliating treatment likely to affect health or safety.

6.4 Workplace Harassment: Harassment may be verbal, non-verbal, written, visual, physical, or digital, and includes emails, messages, calls, social media posts, jokes, comments, displays, or any conduct that violates the dignity of a person.

7. Complaint Mechanism

7.1 Who Can Complain:

  • Any aggrieved woman as defined under this Policy may make a complaint.
  • Where the aggrieved woman is unable to make a complaint due to physical or mental incapacity, or any other reason as permitted by law, her legal heir, relative, friend, co-worker, or any person with knowledge of the incident may file a complaint on her behalf, subject to statutory requirements.

7.2 Time Limit:

  • A complaint should be made within three (3) months from the date of the incident, or in case of a series of incidents, within three months from the date of the last incident.
  • The IC may extend this time limit by a further period not exceeding three (3) months if it is satisfied that circumstances prevented the complainant from filing the complaint in time.

7.3 Mode of Complaint: The complaint shall be in writing and may be submitted by hand/post to any IC member, or by email to the official POSH/HR email: {{ posh_policy.hr_email }}.

8. Complaint Handling Process

A. Receipt of Complaint:

  1. The IC (or Presiding Officer) will acknowledge receipt of the complaint in writing or via email within two (2) working days.
  2. The IC will record the complaint, assign a reference number, and maintain a confidential file.

B. Notice to Respondent:

  1. A copy of the written complaint shall be provided to the respondent within seven (7) working days of receipt of the complaint by the IC.
  2. The respondent shall file a written reply, along with any supporting documents and names of witnesses, within ten (10) working days of receiving the complaint.

C. Conciliation (Optional): Before initiating the inquiry, at the request of the complainant, the IC may attempt conciliation between the parties, provided that no monetary settlement shall be made the basis of such conciliation. If settled, the IC records terms, provides copies, and forwards to HR. No further inquiry is then conducted.

D. Inquiry Process: The IC shall conduct a fair, impartial, and timely inquiry. A minimum of three (3) members, including the Presiding Officer, shall be present. No legal practitioner is permitted. If either party fails to appear for three consecutive hearings, the IC may proceed ex parte or terminate the inquiry after final notice.

E. Interim Relief: During inquiry, the IC may recommend transfer, leave up to 3 months, or temporary work-from-home, which HR shall implement.

F. Inquiry Timeline and Report: The IC shall complete the inquiry within ninety (90) days, prepare a written report within ten (10) days of completion, and provide copies to both parties.

G. Action on Inquiry Report: If not proven, no action is taken. If proven, the IC recommends suitable disciplinary action (warning, withholding promotion, transfer, suspension, or termination). The Company shall act on recommendations within sixty (60) days.

H. Malicious or False Complaints: If a complaint is found to be malicious or false, the IC may recommend disciplinary action against the complainant. Mere inability to substantiate does not imply malice.

9. Confidentiality

All proceedings, records, and documents related to complaints and inquiries under this Policy shall be treated as strictly confidential. Any person who breaches confidentiality may be subject to disciplinary action.

10. Protection Against Retaliation

{{ posh_policy.company_name }} prohibits any form of retaliation, victimization, or adverse action against the complainant, witnesses, or support persons. Any retaliatory act will be treated as a serious disciplinary violation.

11. Record Keeping and Reporting

The IC shall maintain a register of complaints, inquiry proceedings, and actions taken securely for at least five (5) years. The IC shall prepare and submit an Annual Report each calendar year to Management and the District Officer in {{ posh_policy.district_name }}.

12. Awareness and Training

The Company shall conduct regular awareness programs, include POSH awareness in onboarding, conduct annual IC training, and display this policy at conspicuous places.

13. Escalation and External Remedies

An aggrieved woman dissatisfied with findings may appeal to the competent authority/tribunal within the prescribed limit. Escalations may be directed to the HR Head, {{ posh_policy.hr_head_name }}.

14. Amendment and Review

This Policy shall be reviewed at least once every year to ensure compliance with applicable laws.
