Child Protection Policy

POCSO (Protection of Children from Sexual Offences) Compliance Policy

1. Introduction
This policy is framed in strict adherence to "The Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012". Our institution maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards any form of child abuse or neglect.

2. Scope & Applicability
This policy applies to all student, faculty, administrative personnel, contractors, and volunteers who interact with children (individuals under 18 years of age) within our premises or during organizational activities.

3. Code of Conduct
- Student must maintain professional boundaries at all times.
- Avoid being alone with a child in unmonitored or private spaces.
- Use language that is age-appropriate and respectful.
- Physical contact should be avoided except in emergencies or for safety.

4. Mandatory Reporting Requirement
Under Section 19 of the POCSO Act, any person who has apprehension that a sexual offence has been committed, or is likely to be committed, has a legal obligation to report it to the Special Juvenile Police Unit or the local police. Failure to report is a punishable offence.

5. Recruitment & Vetting
All student undergo mandatory background verification and must complete the POCSO Awareness Training before being granted access to child-related zones.
